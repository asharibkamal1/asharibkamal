## Part 1 — 12 ways developers earn online

| # | Path | Time to first $ | Effort to scale | Best if you… |
|---|------|-----------------|-----------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Freelance projects** (platforms or direct clients) | Weeks | Linear (trading time) | want income fast |
| 2 | **Niche freelancing** (e.g. ".NET modernisation") | Weeks | Higher rates, fewer clients | have deep experience in one area |
| 3 | **Remote contract / full-time role** | 1–3 months | Stable | want a predictable salary |
| 4 | **Technical consulting / code reviews** | Weeks | High hourly rate | have senior-level judgement |
| 5 | **Micro-SaaS** (small paid tool) | Months | Compounds | enjoy building products |
| 6 | **Templates & starter kits** (boilerplates, admin dashboards) | 1–2 months | Passive once built | build similar things often |
| 7 | **E-books & guides** | 1–2 months | Passive | like explaining things in writing |
| 8 | **Online courses** | 2–4 months | Passive, high value | can teach step by step |
| 9 | **Newsletter + sponsorships** | 6–12 months | Compounds with audience | write consistently |
| 10 | **Blog + SEO + affiliates** | 6–12 months | Compounds with traffic | are patient |
| 11 | **YouTube / video content** | 6–12 months | Compounds | are comfortable on camera |
| 12 | **Paid community / mentoring** | Months | Grows with audience | enjoy helping people one-to-one |

**Rule of thumb:** start with one *fast* path (1–4) to bring in cash, and one *compounding* path (5–12) to build long-term income.

## Part 2 — Pick your path in 10 minutes

Answer honestly:

1. **How many hours a week can I commit for 6 months?** ______
   - Under 5 → start with #4 or #6
   - 5–15 → one fast + one compounding path
   - 15+ → you can run a product and content in parallel
2. **What do people already ask me for help with?** ______
   → This is your niche.
3. **Do I prefer building, writing, or talking?**
   - Building → #1, #2, #5, #6
   - Writing → #7, #9, #10
   - Talking/teaching → #4, #8, #11, #12
4. **Do I need money this quarter, or am I building for next year?**
   - This quarter → fast path first
   - Next year → compounding path first

## Part 3 — Your 30-day plan

### Week 1 — Foundation
- [ ] Choose your niche in one sentence: "I help ___ do ___."
- [ ] Update LinkedIn headline and About to match it
- [ ] Create a one-page portfolio (3 projects, contact link)

### Week 2 — Visibility
- [ ] Publish 3 posts about problems you've solved (LinkedIn or blog)
- [ ] Join 2 communities where your future clients/readers are
- [ ] Help 5 people for free with real answers

### Week 3 — First offer
- [ ] Define one clear offer with a price (e.g. "API integration — $X, 2 weeks")
- [ ] Send 10 personal messages / proposals per day on weekdays
- [ ] For a product path: publish a landing page + waitlist

### Week 4 — First dollar
- [ ] Follow up on every conversation
- [ ] Deliver the first job extremely well; ask for a testimonial
- [ ] Review: what worked? Double down on it next month

## Part 4 — Mistakes to avoid

- Trying five paths at once
- Building a product for months without talking to a single user
- Competing on price instead of specialising
- Waiting until you "feel ready"
- Not collecting emails from day one
